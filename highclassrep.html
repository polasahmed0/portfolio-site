The challenges

HighClassRep needed stronger visibility across a broad and highly competitive fashion catalog.

The site covers many commercial categories, which creates a large SEO opportunity but also makes keyword targeting, category relevance, and internal structure more important.

01

Highly competitive fashion searches

The website competes across sneakers, winter jackets, bags, clothing, boots, tracksuits, high heels, jewellery, accessories, and men’s and women’s fashion.

02

Large e-commerce product catalog

Many products and categories needed a clear SEO structure so Google could understand how individual product pages connect to broader commercial sections.

03

Category and product visibility needed improvement

The site needed stronger optimisation around commercial intent so more products and category pages could gain visibility beyond a small set of terms.

04

Ongoing search monitoring was essential

With such a broad catalog, Search Console data needed to guide which queries, pages, and categories should receive further optimisation.

What we did

Built the SEO strategy around products, categories, and commercial search intent.

The work focused on improving page relevance, keyword targeting, internal linking, and search visibility across the store’s key fashion categories.

✓

E-commerce keyword optimization

Commercial searches around sneakers, designer footwear, fashion products, jackets, bags, boots, accessories, and men’s and women’s fashion were matched to relevant pages.

✓

Product & category optimization

Titles, headings, product descriptions, category content, keyword placement, and page structure were improved to strengthen relevance.

✓

Internal linking and site architecture

Connections between categories, products, and related commercial sections were improved so search engines could understand the store more clearly.

✓

Search performance monitoring

Search Console data was used to identify growing queries, impression gains, ranking movement, click opportunities, and pages needing further work.

SEO results & performance

Search visibility expanded over the long term and continued improving in recent periods.

The available Search Console reports show strong organic traffic, high click-through rates, and better ranking performance across recent comparison periods.
